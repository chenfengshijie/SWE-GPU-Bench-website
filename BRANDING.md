# SWE-GPU-Bench visual identity

## Mascot

A cheerful alpaca GPU engineer, inspired by the friendly research mascots of [SWE-bench](https://github.com/SWE-bench/SWE-bench) and [SWE-Perf](https://swe-perf.github.io/). This is a newly generated illustration, not an official logo or an endorsement by either project. The reference artwork is not distributed in this repository.

- Transparent original: `public/assets/gpu-llama.png`
- Web display derivative: `public/assets/gpu-llama.webp`
- Browser icon: `public/assets/favicon.png`
- The wordmark is HTML text, with GPU highlighted in teal.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 7 November 2026. Reference images were the SWE-bench alpaca banner and the SWE-Perf alpaca-and-rocket banner.

Generation brief: create a new companion-style academic benchmark mascot with a fluffy golden-cream alpaca, tall pink-centered ears, a rounded forelock, smiling closed eyes, rosy cheeks, and dark brown hooves. Seat the alpaca in a relaxed pose holding a teal GPU graphics card with two dark navy fans and gold edge contacts, with a small wrench in the other hoof and teal safety goggles on its forehead. Use soft flat illustration, broad rounded shapes, peach shadows, minimal outlines, a transparent background, and no wordmark, laptop, rocket, or other branding. Preserve the references' friendly spirit while making GPU engineering the distinctive subject.

## Palette and typography

- Teal: `#078675`

- Pale mint: `#eff9f6`

The font license files are in `public/assets/fonts/`. Fonts are served locally. The existing `chip.svg` is retained as an alternative technical symbol.
